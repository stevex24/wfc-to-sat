Direct answers

  1. Frequency comparison: ordinary/SAT pooled tile KL is 0.14149/0.149088; pooled edge KL is 1.53216/1.53551. The aggregate distributions are close, but no paired output is exactly identical.
  2. Context comparison: ordinary/SAT pooled tile KL is 0.0331538/0.0300366; pooled edge KL is 0.0518559/0.048626. The aggregate distributions are also close.
  3. Exact matches: Frequency has 0/500; Context has 13/500 (seed 0).
  4. Conflicts when images differ: Frequency has 500 conflict/backtracking runs among 500 differences. Context has 446 among 487; its other 41 differences occurred without a conflict. Difference alone therefore does not prove backtracking.
  5. Context advantage: SAT preserves the strong pooled resemblance advantage, especially edge KL (0.048626 Context versus 1.53551 Frequency). Mean and median edge KL agree. Per-run tile KL is more variable and is not uniformly better.
  6. Seed 0 representativeness: Frequency SAT tile/edge KL is 0.191063/1.76743; Context SAT is 0.390953/0.534522. Both lie within their observed distributions. Context seed 0 is better than its medians but is not an extreme best case.
  7. Timeouts: 0/500 Frequency; 0/500 Context. Frequency has a longer solve-time tail (maximum 9.296s) than Context (0.433s).

These are matched-seed and distributional comparisons, not a claim that ordinary WFC and WFC-as-SAT are equivalent algorithms.
