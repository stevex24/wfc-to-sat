Simple Knot · Moore is worse

Context-8 degrades every reported mean relative to Context-4. SAT fallback rises from 6.6% to 41.6%, with conflicts increasing from 11 to 34.

What the experiment supports

  • Moore context is not consistently superior; its effect depends on the image.
  • Letter-Z diagonal statistics improve slightly, without a clear visual Z reconstruction.
  • The larger context often increases unseen-context fallback, though not on letter-Z.
  • Stick’s Context-4 result remains stronger on horizontal/vertical resemblance; Moore is not an upgrade there.
  • Conflict-free Stick outputs match between ordinary WFC and SAT. Under conflicts, Moore’s CDCL interaction varies sharply by image.
  • Moore looks worth targeted follow-up on diagonal-rich inputs, not adoption as a universal default.
Modest matched samples: 20 seeds for Stick and letter-Z; 5 for Cat, Knot, and Simple Knot. No significance tests.
